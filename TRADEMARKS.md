# LayerHound name and logo

LayerHound's **code** is open source under the [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). The **LayerHound name, logo, mascot and wordmark** are not covered by that license. They belong to Team Tactical RC and identify the official LayerHound software and boards.

This covers:
- the "LayerHound" name, and "LayerHound by Team Tactical RC"
- the hound mascot, the logo and the wordmark, including the artwork in `public/brand/` and `docs/brand/`
- the logo inlays of the case in `hardware/case/` (`emblem.py` and the `case-logo-*` files)

## What's fine without asking

- Using and modifying the code under the AGPL, including running your own copy.
- Saying your project is "based on LayerHound" or "compatible with LayerHound".
- Writing about LayerHound, reviewing it, or teaching with it, using the name to refer to it.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a modified version, or a product or service, under the LayerHound name or logo. Forks should use their own name and remove or replace the logo artwork.
- Selling boards, cases or kits that carry the LayerHound name or logo.
- Using the mascot or logo in a way that suggests Team Tactical RC made or endorses something it didn't.

To ask, contact Team Tactical RC.
