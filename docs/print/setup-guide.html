Set up your LayerHound

Watch every printer in your farm from one dashboard, on your own network.
About 10 minutes, no programming needed.

You'll need: your LayerHound and its power supply, a phone or computer on your home network, and an Ethernet cable if you have one (Wi-Fi works too).

1 Connect it

With a cable: plug LayerHound into your router with an Ethernet cable, then plug in the power. Wait about 2 minutes.

No cable? Plug in the power and wait a minute or two. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network LayerHound-Setup. A setup page opens: pick your home Wi-Fi and enter its password. Then reconnect your phone to your home Wi-Fi.

2 Open the dashboard

On a phone or computer on the same network, open:

http://layerhound.local

or scan the code.

If it doesn't open, see the tips below.

3 Make it yours

  • Name your farm. You can change it later.
  • Create your admin password. There's no default password. Use at least 8 characters and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Choose whether to share anonymous usage stats. Nothing is sent unless you say yes.
  • Save your recovery key. It sets a new password if you ever forget yours. Write it here and keep this sheet somewhere safe:

4 Add your printers

LayerHound looks for printers on your network by itself. They show up on the Dashboard as printers found on your network:

  • Klipper: press Add.
  • Bambu Lab: type the printer's access code, from its screen under network or WLAN settings.
  • OctoPrint: press Allow in OctoPrint, then Allow in OctoPrint.

Not listed? Use Print Farm → Add printer.

Good to know

  • layerhound.local won't open? Make sure you're on the same network as LayerHound. Some Android phones and Windows PCs can't open .local names: find "layerhound" in your router's device list and open http:// followed by its IP address.
  • Before unplugging, use Settings → About & maintenance → Shut down board, then wait until the activity light stops blinking.
  • Away from home: never open LayerHound to the internet with port forwarding. Use a private network app such as Tailscale.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password? and enter your recovery key.
  • Questions or ideas? Use Send feedback in the dashboard menu. It goes straight to Team Tactical RC.
