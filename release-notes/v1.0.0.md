LayerHound 1.0 is the first public release of the print farm and home lab dashboard by Team Tactical RC. It watches your 3D printers and the small server it runs on, all in one place on your own network.

## Install

On a Debian-based board (Debian 12, Armbian, Raspberry Pi OS or Ubuntu), run:

    curl -fsSL https://github.com/TeamTacticalRC/layerhound-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash

Then open http://layerhound.local and create your admin account. The installer checks that the download is signed by Team Tactical RC before installing.

## What's in 1.0

**Your printers**
- Klipper (Moonraker), Bambu Lab (P1, X1, A1) and OctoPrint printers on one dashboard.
- For each printer: progress, time left, nozzle and bed temperatures, and layer count.
- Part thumbnails, and live cameras: Bambu P1 and A1, Klipper webcams, or any camera address.
- A network scan finds printers and fills in the Add printer form for you.
- Print history: every print, success rate, hours printed per day, and filament used. Klipper printers' past jobs are imported.

**Your server and network**
- Processor, memory, temperature, storage and network for the board, with history.
- Drive health and a shared LayerHound Files folder.
- Internet uptime, monitored devices, and Wi-Fi settings, all without a terminal.
- Health checks for your other apps, with Home Assistant and Pi-hole stats.
- Alerts when a printer, device, service or the internet goes down.

**Easy setup**
- No cable needed: a new board creates a "LayerHound-Setup" Wi-Fi network. Join it from your phone to connect the board to your Wi-Fi and create your account.
- One-line installer, and one-click updates in Settings, signed by Team Tactical RC. If an update doesn't start, the previous version comes back by itself.

**Accounts and security**
- Sign-in is required, with no default passwords.
- Admin and view-only accounts.
- Read-only access keys for devices like an LED status bar.
- Optional viewing without sign-in on your own network, for a shop wall screen.
- Printer access codes and tokens are stored encrypted.

**Everything else**
- Light and dark mode, chosen per device.
- Six accent colors, °C or °F, and 12- or 24-hour time.
- Case fan speed that follows the board's temperature (on the LayerHound board).
- Backup and restore of your setup.
- Send feedback from inside the dashboard.

## Good to know

- **Your data stays home.** LayerHound only contacts the internet to check its connection, once a day for updates (you can turn this off), and when you send feedback.
- **Printers are watched, not controlled.** LayerHound never starts, stops or changes prints.
- **Bambu Lab printers** allow only one local connection at a time. Newer Bambu firmware may need LAN Only mode, and on some models Developer mode. Bambu has been limiting third-party access, so a future Bambu firmware could stop LayerHound from reading Bambu printers.
- **Bambu printers** don't report filament use, and their print history is recorded from when LayerHound starts watching.
- **Never expose LayerHound to the internet.** For remote access, use a private network tool such as Tailscale.

## Help and feedback

The owner's guide covers adding printers, accounts, Wi-Fi, updates, backups and troubleshooting: https://github.com/TeamTacticalRC/LayerHound/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md Forgot your password? Run "layerhound reset-password" on the board. Ideas or problems: use Send feedback in the dashboard menu.
