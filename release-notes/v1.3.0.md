LayerHound 1.3 lets you reach your farm from anywhere, pause or start prints from the dashboard, and get back in if you forget your password.

## New

**Remote access with Tailscale**
- Settings → Remote access → **Connect with Tailscale**: sign in to your own free Tailscale account (or scan the code on your phone), and LayerHound shows its private remote address with a QR code.
- Install the Tailscale app on your phone, sign in with the same account, and open your dashboard from anywhere. Nothing is opened to the internet, and only your own devices can reach it.

**Printer controls (optional, off by default)**
- Settings → Printer controls: **pause, resume and cancel** prints from a printer's panel. Handy when an alert reaches your phone and a print needs stopping.
- Turn on **Also allow starting prints** to start a file already on a Klipper or OctoPrint printer (searchable, newest first or A–Z), or upload one. You confirm the bed is clear first, with the camera shown.
- Admins only. Every action asks first, and LayerHound only says it worked once the printer confirms it.
- Bambu Lab: pause and cancel only work if the printer is in LAN Only mode with Developer Mode on; LayerHound tells you if the printer ignores it. Starting prints on Bambu isn't supported yet.

**Forgot your password? Use your recovery key**
- New installs show a one-time **recovery key** at the end of setup. If you ever forget your admin password, choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen and enter the key.
- Already set up? The dashboard asks you to make one (also in Settings → Login & users).

**Recent problems**
- Settings → Recent problems lists what went wrong lately in plain words: printers that couldn't be reached, commands that didn't work, updates, and crashes. It stays on your LayerHound.
- When you **Send feedback** about a problem, you can include them, with printer names, addresses and serial numbers left out. Or use **Report a bug on GitHub**, which fills in the details for you.

## Improved

- Small buttons and links across the dashboard now show at their intended size.
- If part of the dashboard page ever crashes, you get a **Reload** button instead of a blank page.

## Updating

This update adds Tailscale to the board, so it installs from the board itself instead of with one click:

1. On the LayerHound board (over SSH, or with a keyboard and screen), run the command shown in **Settings → Updates** (it has a Copy button):
   `curl -fsSL https://github.com/TeamTacticalRC/layerhound-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash -s -- --update`
2. Type your password when asked. It takes a few minutes.

Your printers, settings and accounts are kept, the database is backed up first, and the previous version comes back if anything goes wrong.

**Docker:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` (remote access in Docker: use Tailscale on that machine).

Owner's guide: https://github.com/TeamTacticalRC/LayerHound/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md
